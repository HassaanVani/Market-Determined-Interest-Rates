# Specification 0.3 data dictionary

## Empirical inputs

All bank levels are reported by the FDIC in thousands of nominal dollars. The
model later normalizes the median sampled bank's deposits to 100; model units are
never labeled dollars.

| Canonical field | Source | Construction |
|---|---|---|
| `bank_id` | FDIC `CERT` | FDIC certificate identifier |
| `quarter` | FDIC `REPDTE` | Calendar quarter |
| `assets` | FDIC `ASSET` | Total assets |
| `deposits` | FDIC `DEP` | Total deposits |
| `gross_loans` | FDIC `LNLSGR` | Gross loans and leases |
| `liquid_assets` | FDIC `CHBAL` | Cash and balances due |
| `equity` | FDIC `EQ` | Total equity capital |
| `ci_loans` | FDIC `LNCI` | Commercial and industrial loans |
| `ci_chargeoffs` | FDIC `NTCI` | Net C&I charge-offs |
| `effective_rate` | FR 2028D A16/A19 | Weighted rate, divided by 100 |
| `loan_amount` | FR 2028D A16/A19 | Reported balance × 1,000 / loan count |
| `maturity_months` | FR 2028D A16/A19 | Weighted average maturity |
| `prime_spread` | FR 2028D/FRED | Effective rate less quarterly DPRIME |
| `loan_amount_2025q4` | FR 2028D/CPI-U | Nominal amount × 2025Q4 CPI / quarter CPI |

Derived bank ratios are `equity/assets`, `gross_loans/deposits`,
`liquid_assets/deposits`, `ci_loans/gross_loans`, `ci_chargeoffs/ci_loans`, and
within-bank quarterly deposit and loan growth.

The executable alias dictionary is
`configs/v0.3/data_dictionary.yaml`. Missing required fields, quarters, or unique
bank-quarter keys are fatal. Impossible rows are logged before 1/99
winsorization within quarter and asset-size tercile. Both equal-bank and
asset-weighted moments are retained.

## Simulation event tables

The authoritative schema is executable in `v03/schema.py`. The principal keys
are `run_id`, `period`, stable application/offer/loan IDs, and agent IDs.

- `experiment_runs`: canonical configuration, four seeds, status, timing, shard,
  source/calibration/code/package fingerprints, and Git commit.
- `credit_applications`: purpose, maturity, expected return, borrower state, and
  requested/approved/accepted/unfunded quantities.
- `bank_offers`: approved quantity, rejection code, maturity, nominal quote, and
  every pricing component.
- `loan_contracts` and `loan_events`: amortization, interest, default, deposit
  recovery, collateral recovery, and write-off history.
- `firm_states`: deposits, debt, capital, equity, investment, labor, wages,
  output, inventory, sales, credit, and debt-service burden.
- `bank_states`: reserves, deposits, loans, interbank and emergency positions,
  equity, RWA, capital/liquidity ratios, profit, market share, status, and costs.
- `period_macro`: production, investment, consumption, inventory, rationing,
  rate dispersion, defaults, liquidity shortfall, bank status, and resolution.
- `bank_resolution_events`: prior/required equity, injection, money issuance,
  resolution cost, and state transition.
