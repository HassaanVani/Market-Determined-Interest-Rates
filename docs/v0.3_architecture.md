# Specification 0.3 architecture

Status: implemented, pre-confirmatory, not frozen. Version 0.2 remains the
immutable exploratory specification.

## Research boundary

Version 0.3 is an institutional credit-market experiment. It compares a policy
benchmark with attenuated local-state pass-through against an endogenous funding
benchmark with full pass-through. It does not identify an optimal US policy rate
or make a quantitative US forecast.

The treatment changes the pricing map only. Both regimes share borrower state,
bank balance sheets, prudential feasibility, settlement, shocks, and real-side
technology. Baseline quote intercepts are fitted before the confirmatory freeze
to prevent a mechanical mean-rate treatment.

## Period sequence

1. Complete a pending one-period bank resolution and record authority money.
2. Depreciate capital, pay wages, and produce with Cobb–Douglas technology.
3. Create loan applications and modular partial offers.
4. Accept cheapest compatible offers subject to lender concentration.
5. Split funded credit between investment and working capital.
6. Clear consumption subject to goods supply and allocate sales by output.
7. Service and amortize each loan; record deposit and collateral recovery.
8. Clear interbank and emergency liquidity; retain any end-of-day shortfall.
9. Mark insolvent banks for resolution and record all firm, bank, and macro states.
10. Check deposits, credit, finite values, foreign keys, and horizon completeness.

Payments are final under provisional intraday settlement. Emergency loans are
liquidity liabilities. Resolution injections add reserves and equity and are
separately recorded as base-money issuance and fiscal resolution cost.

## Treatment and outcome invariants

- A shared named seed bundle drives environment, matching, shocks, and behavior.
- Relationship pricing effects are stable hashes of the matching seed and IDs.
- Applications record requested, approved, accepted, and unfunded principal.
- Offers record benchmark, fixed spread, risk, liquidity, capital, inflation,
  bank effect, and local pass-through.
- Firm equity is deposits plus productive capital less debt.
- Bank equity is a balance-sheet residual and cannot conceal a resolution transfer.
- Economic inference is across matched seeds, never agent-period rows.

## Current acceptance state

The real 2022Q1–2024Q4 calibration fit passes the normalized-RMSE threshold. The
first proper 2025 dynamic holdout attempt does not yet pass the six-of-eight
moment-group gate. This is an intentional hard stop: pilot, freeze, and
confirmatory evidence must not be represented as journal-ready until the model
revision passes without dropping targets.
